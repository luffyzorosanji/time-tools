What is BMI?

BMI (Body Mass Index) is a measurement of body fat based on your height and weight. It helps you understand if your weight is healthy for your height.

BMI Categories

Underweight: BMI below 18.5. Normal weight: BMI 18.5 to 24.9. Overweight: BMI 25 to 29.9. Obese: BMI 30 and above.

How to Calculate BMI?

BMI is calculated by dividing your weight in kg by your height in metres squared. Our free online BMI calculator does this automatically for you in seconds.
