📏 How to Take Your Measurements

Use a measuring tape. Measure in inches. Keep the tape snug but not tight.

🔵

Chest

Wrap the tape around the fullest part of your chest, under your armpits and across your shoulder blades. Keep arms relaxed at your sides.

🟢

Neck

Measure around the base of your neck where a collar would sit. Add 0.5 inch for comfort. This is your collar size.

🟡

Arm Length

Measure from the top of your shoulder, down to your elbow, and then to your wrist. Keep arm slightly bent.

🔴

Waist

Measure around your natural waist — the narrowest part of your torso, about 1 inch above your belly button.

🟣

Hip / Seat

Measure around the fullest part of your hips and buttocks, usually about 8 inches below your natural waist.

🟠

Inseam / Length

Measure from your crotch down to your ankle bone along the inside of your leg. This is your trouser length.

💡 Fit Guide — Which Fit is Right for You?

✂️

Slim Fit

Hugs the body closely. Best for lean or athletic body types. Looks sharp and modern. Order 1 size up if you have broader shoulders.

👔

Regular Fit

Classic fit with comfortable room throughout. Best for average body types. Good for formal and casual wear. Most popular choice.

😌

Loose / Relaxed Fit

Extra room throughout. Best for larger body types or those who prefer comfort. Good for casual wear and summer.

Men's Clothing Size Guide for Online Shopping in India

Online shopping for clothes in India can be confusing because different brands like Myntra, Amazon, Flipkart, H&M, Zara, and Ajio all use different size charts. Our free men's size finder helps you calculate your exact shirt size and trouser size based on your actual body measurements.

The most common problem men face is not knowing their neck size, chest size, arm length for shirts, or waist and inseam for trousers. By entering your measurements above, you can shop confidently without worrying about returns.

How to Find Your Shirt Size in India

Indian shirt sizes are usually given as numbers like 38, 40, 42, 44 which refer to the chest measurement in centimeters. International sizes use XS, S, M, L, XL, XXL. Our calculator converts between both systems so you always order the right size.

How to Find Your Trouser Size for Online Shopping

Trouser sizes in India are usually given as waist size in inches like 28, 30, 32, 34. Always check your waist and hip measurements before ordering. For the best fit, also check the inseam length to make sure the trouser length is correct for your height.
